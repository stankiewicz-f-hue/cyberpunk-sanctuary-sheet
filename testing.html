    How it plays
    • The first pick comes from the highlighted top row. After that the picks alternate: a column, then a row, then a column again. A cell can only be used once.
    • Picked codes drop into the buffer in order. A sequence uploads once its codes sit back to back, in order, anywhere in the buffer.
    • Sequences can overlap in the buffer, which is how a small buffer covers a lot.
    • The breach ends when the buffer is full, time runs out, or nothing else can be uploaded. Each uploaded sequence is a NET action that succeeds.
    • Every board is built around a real winning path, so a perfect run always exists. Show solution after the breach reveals it.
    • Keyboard: arrow keys move between the highlighted cells, Enter picks.
    • Board scaling by DV: 6 or less is 5×5 with two sequences, 8 is 5×5 with three, 10 to 12 is 6×6 with three, 14 and up is 7×7 with four. From DV 12 every sequence is one code longer, from DV 16 two.
    • Buffer starts at 3 plus half the Interface rank, rounded up. The d10 moves it: beat the DV by 5 or more for +1 slot and +10 s, miss the DV for -1 slot, miss by 5 or more for -1 slot and -10 s. A natural 10 gives +1 slot and +15 s, a natural 1 takes them away.
    • Full dive: turns the check into several floors deep instead of one board, getting harder as you go. From Black Site up, a floor can spawn named Black ICE with its own health bar — land Zap to damage it, or leave it standing and it hits back when you jack out of that floor.
    • From Corporate Grid up, the architecture forks after the first floor. Only one branch leads to the bottom floor, where the Virus gets uploaded. Pick a wrong branch and you fight a dead-end floor before choosing again. Landing Pathfinder on the first floor marks the right branch. Landing Slide carries you straight past the fork. On dives without a fork, Pathfinder previews the next floor and Slide skips it.
    • Unplug jacks you out of the current floor at any time and ends the dive, keeping what you already landed. Live ICE still gets a parting shot.
