USING THIS SHEET

Click a stat name, a skill's BASE number, or an attack's ATK / DMG button. Results land in the ROLL LOG (bottom right).

MOD adds a situational bonus or penalty to every roll. DV is the target number; the log then tells you SUCCESS or FAIL. SPEND LUCK adds that many points to your next roll and takes them from your Luck.

Wound penalties are added for you.

Multiclassing: choose up to 3 roles at the top. Each one shows its own ability card and rank. This is a house-rule option, so agree it with your GM.

THE BASIC ROLL

d10 + STAT + SKILL (+ mods). Meet or beat the DV to succeed.

Natural 10: roll another d10 and add it. Natural 1: roll another d10 and subtract it. The sheet does both automatically.

COMBAT

Initiative: d10 + REF, highest goes first.

Attack: d10 + REF + weapon skill against the DV (set by range, or the target's Evasion roll). Use the Weapons tab: pick a weapon type, then press FIRE and DMG.

Armor: subtract the SP of the hit location from damage. If damage gets through, that armor loses 1 SP.

Ammo: FIRE spends 1 round, AUTOFIRE spends 10, RELOAD refills the magazine from your reserve. Autofire damage works differently, so check your book.

WOUNDS & DEATH

Seriously Wounded (half HP or less): −2 to all actions.

Mortally Wounded (0 HP): −4 to all actions, −6 MOVE (min 1), and a Death Save each turn: roll d10, you live only if it is lower than your BODY.

IMPROVEMENT POINTS

Your GM awards IP each session: type them in with ADD IP. A skill level costs 20 × the new level (×2 skills cost double). A role rank costs 60 × the new rank. IP does not raise stats. The two cost boxes can be changed for house rules.

LUCK

Spend Luck before you roll: each point adds +1. Your pool refills each session. Rules here follow the core book as summarised; your GM has the final say.

COST PER LEVEL

Press ▲ next to a skill or a role rank to buy its next level. The cost is on the button and comes out of AVAILABLE. Levels you type in by hand are free (character creation).

CLANKER CHASSIS

Clanker drawbacks: (1) people treat you as property, so you always carry at least stigma level 1 (see Visible Weapons on the Weapons tab); (2) POWER only lasts 3 days, see the rest tracker above; (3) no natural healing, so the +1, +5 and FULL buttons are hidden and only REPAIR restores SDP; (4) upkeep costs money.

Clanker mode turns HP into SDP, healing into repair and the death save into a reboot. Picking a chassis fills in armor and the SDP bonus (these are homebrew presets, change them freely). REPAIR rolls the chosen skill (against the DV box if set), then restores the dice in SDP and spends the parts cost from your Eurobucks. Set DICE to 0 or the parts to 0 to skip either. REBOOT is the BODY roll at 0 SDP. Humanity shows as Integrity while this mode is on.

RESTED

Organic: 7 days of sleep. 5 left or fewer is −2 to all rolls, 3 or fewer is −4, 1 is −6 and 0 is out. Clanker: 3 days of power. 2 left is −2, 1 is −4 and 0 is powered off. At 0, rolls are blocked until you rest or recharge. Set the max to 0 to switch the rule off.

STREET CRED

LEVEL 0

Street Cred is a total of points and each level costs more than the one before. BASE COST is what level 1 costs, and every level beyond the first adds the base cost again: with 20 that is 20, 40, 60, 80… so 20, 60, 120, 200 points in total. NEXT LEVEL COSTS shows what the level you are working towards costs and updates as you level. Level 50 is the cap. Losing points can drop you a level.

NO STIGMA

Set a weapon to EQUIPPED and mark whether it is CONCEALABLE. Every equipped weapon that is not concealable is on show. Each one beyond ALLOWED raises your STIGMA LEVEL (clankers start one level higher). There is no roll penalty: instead the world reacts, getting crueler at each level. Press ENTER A PUBLIC PLACE to roll how people respond this time. Unequip a weapon to stow it out of sight.

STABLE

Max Humanity is EMP × 10. Every full 10 points lost costs 1 EMP, and the sheet applies that to EMP skills automatically. Press INSTALL on a location to add that foundation, then pick the location on each option and how many slots it uses. Type dice like 2d6 and press ROLL to fill in the loss.

THERAPY & EVENTS

Therapy restores lost Humanity (never above your max) and takes it off your LOST total. Event loss is Humanity the GM takes during traumatic scenes. The cost is paid from your Eurobucks (set it to 0 to skip). The BASIC and INTENSIVE presets (500 eb / 2d6 and 1000 eb / 4d6) come from a community summary of the book, so check them and edit freely.

HOW A RUN WORKS

  1. 1JACK IN1 Net action
  2. 2BREACH THE FLOOR1 Net action
  3. 3GO DEEPER1 Net action per floor
  4. 4UNPLUG1 Net action

Set Interface and Difficulty and roll your d10. The big button jacks you in first; press it again to start the breach. Every move to a deeper floor costs a Net action, so plan your turns.

Sequences on the board

RECENT RUNS

    HOW IT PLAYS
    • Net actions: Jack In, starting the breach, every move to a deeper floor (Descend, Slide or a fork door) and Unplug each cost 1 Net action. You get 2 at Interface 1 to 3, 3 at 4 to 6, 4 at 7 to 9 and 5 at 10, unless you type your own total in the Quickhacks panel. END TURN refreshes them.
    • Damage: when ICE is still standing as you jack out, or a breach locks you out, the hit is rolled for you and taken straight off your HP (armor skipped). ICE hits 2d6 at Black Site, 3d6 at Militech and 4d6 at Arasaka, half as many dice if you wounded it. A failed breach costs 1d6, or 2d6 from Black Site up. The GM can waive a hit with one tap.
    • Virus payload: land the Virus on the bottom floor and you can write what you rewrite in the system. Your party sees it in the Roll Log.
    • The first pick comes from the highlighted top row. After that the picks alternate: a column, then a row, then a column again. A cell can only be used once.
    • Picked codes drop into the buffer in order. A sequence uploads once its codes sit back to back, in order, anywhere in the buffer. Sequences can overlap, which is how a small buffer covers a lot.
    • The breach ends when the buffer is full, time runs out, or nothing else can be uploaded. Each uploaded sequence is a NET action that succeeds.
    • Every board is built around a real winning path, so a perfect run always exists. Show solution reveals it once the breach is over.
    • Keyboard: arrow keys move between the highlighted cells, Enter picks.
    • Board scaling by DV: up to 8 is 5×5 with three sequences, 9 to 12 is 6×6 with four, 13 and up is 7×7 with five. From DV 12 every sequence is one code longer, from DV 16 two. Higher DV also means less time.
    • Buffer starts at 2 plus half the Interface rank, rounded up (3 to 9 slots). The check is Interface + d10 + your sheet modifiers against the DV. Beat it by 5 or more: +1 slot, +10 s. Miss it: −1 slot, −15 s. Miss by 5 or more: −2 slots, −25 s. A natural 10 adds +1 slot and +15 s, a natural 1 takes away 2 slots and 25 s.
    • From your sheet: Interface is your Netrunner rank. Wounds, sleep or power, the MOD box and your Interface bonus are added to the check, ROLL D10 spends the SPEND LUCK box, and every result lands in your Roll Log and in this character's run log. At 0 sleep or power the sheet blocks rolls, and so does this.
    • From the Quickhacks panel: the target and Passwall DV come across, Backdoor is put on the board, and landing it marks the Passwall breached on the sheet.
    • Full dive: turns the check into several floors deep instead of one board, each floor 2 DV harder. From Black Site up, a floor can spawn named Black ICE with its own health bar: land Zap to destroy it, or leave it standing and it hits back when you jack out of that floor.
    • From Corporate Grid up, the architecture forks after the first floor. Only one branch leads to the bottom floor, where the Virus gets uploaded. Pick a wrong branch and you fight a dead-end floor before choosing again. Landing Pathfinder on the first floor marks the right branch. Landing Slide carries you straight past the fork. On dives without a fork, Pathfinder previews the next floor and Slide skips it.
    • Unplug jacks you out of the current floor at any time and ends the dive, keeping what you already landed. Live ICE still gets a parting shot. Leaving this screen pauses the run.
